What this old census repo becomes in 2026

The 2011 Nepal census is frozen history. The craft is not the year — it is turning messy administrative tables into trustworthy geography.

  • Toponym reconciliation Explicit CBS ↔ GADM aliases so Chitwan/Chitawan and Kavre/Kavrepalanchok join cleanly.
  • Aggregate-before-rate VDC counts are summed first; literacy and service rates are rebuilt at district scale.
  • Geodesic density Population density uses WGS84 ellipsoid area, not planar degree math.
  • Cartographic delivery Quantile class breaks, simplified polygons, MapLibre choropleth with live district inspection.
